Geographic Information

Location, land area, and topography of Las Nieves

Location

The Municipality of Las Nieves is located in the province of Agusan del Norte, Philippines. It is situated in the northeastern part of Mindanao, approximately 1,476 kilometers southeast of Manila.

Topography

The municipality features alluvial plains stretching up to the mountains, with vast agricultural lands and cool weather conditions. The terrain ranges from flat coastal plains to rolling hills and mountainous areas, providing diverse ecosystems and natural resources.

The Agusan River, one of Mindanao's major river systems, flows through the municipality, providing irrigation for farmlands and serving as a vital transportation route for residents.
