Municipal Seal & Logo

The official symbols representing the identity and heritage of Las Nieves

Municipal Seal of Las Nieves

OFFICIAL SEAL • REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES

Symbolism

The Leaf
Represents the rich agricultural resources and lush forests of Las Nieves, as well as the municipality's commitment to environmental sustainability.
The River
Symbolizes the Agusan River, the lifeblood of the municipality, providing transportation, livelihood, and natural beauty to the people.
The Mountains
Represents the ancestral domain of the Manobo and Higaonon people, and the municipality's rich cultural heritage.
The Unity
Symbolizes the unity and cooperation between the indigenous communities and the local government for peace and progress.

Meaning of Elements

  • Circular Shape - Represents the continuous development and unity of the municipality.
  • Stars - Represent the 20 barangays that comprise the Municipality of Las Nieves.
  • Year 1963 - Marks the official founding year of Las Nieves as a municipality.
  • White Colors - Represents "The Snows" (Las Nieves in Spanish), referring to the cold fog that early missionaries encountered.
  • Agricultural Symbols - Represent the primary livelihood of the people - farming and fishing.
  • Church Icon - Honors the municipality's patroness, Nuestra Señora de Las Nieves (Our Lady of the Snow).

Official Municipal Logo

LAS NIEVES • AGUSAN DEL NORTE

  • Green Color - Represents agriculture, environment, and the lush natural resources of Las Nieves.
  • Municipal Hall Icon - Symbolizes governance and public service to the community.
  • Peace Symbol - Represents the municipality's commitment to peace and order.
  • Year 1963 - The official founding year of Las Nieves as a municipality.
