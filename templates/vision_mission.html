Vision, Mission & Core Values

Our commitment to the people of Las Nieves

Vision

A progressive, peaceful, and prosperous Las Nieves where every citizen enjoys a quality life in a sustainable and resilient community, guided by transparent and accountable governance, rooted in our rich cultural heritage as Manobo and Higaonon people.

Mission

To deliver efficient and effective public services, promote agricultural and economic development, protect our natural resources, preserve our cultural traditions, and empower our citizens through participatory governance.

Core Values

Integrity
Upholding honesty and transparency in all government transactions
Excellence
Striving for quality service delivery to every citizen
Unity
Working together as one community for common goals

Strategic Goals

  • Ensure quality education for all youth of Las Nieves
  • Provide accessible healthcare services to all 20 barangays
  • Develop agricultural infrastructure and support farmers
  • Protect the Agusan River and natural environment
  • Promote peace and order throughout the municipality
  • Enhance agricultural productivity and food security
  • Preserve Manobo and Higaonon cultural heritage
  • Improve road networks connecting all barangays
